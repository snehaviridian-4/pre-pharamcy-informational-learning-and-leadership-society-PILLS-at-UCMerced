Our Mission

Who We Are

The Pre-Pharmacy Informational Learning and Leadership Society (PILLS) is a student-run organization at UC Merced dedicated to supporting pre-pharmacy students on their academic and professional journey.

We believe in fostering a supportive community where students can learn, grow, and prepare for success in pharmacy school and beyond.

What We Believe In

  • ✓ Community: Supporting one another through the challenges of pre-pharmacy coursework
  • ✓ Leadership: Developing future pharmacy leaders
  • ✓ Education: Providing resources and mentorship
  • ✓ Professional Development: Preparing for pharmacy school and careers

Our Values

Why Join PILLS?

Networking Opportunities

Connect with pre-pharmacy students, pharmacy professionals, and alumni. Build relationships that will support your career.

Mentorship

Get guidance from experienced students and mentors who have navigated the pre-pharmacy journey successfully.

Academic Support

Participate in study groups and academic events designed to help you excel in pre-pharmacy coursework.

Professional Development

Learn about pharmacy careers, school applications, and develop skills that make you a competitive candidate.

Social Events

Build friendships and enjoy social activities with fellow pre-pharmacy students.

Leadership Experience

Get involved in leadership roles within the organization and develop skills for your future career.
