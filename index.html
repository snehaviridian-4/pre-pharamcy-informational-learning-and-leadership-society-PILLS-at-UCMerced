About Our Club

Our Mission

PILLS is dedicated to providing pre-pharmacy students with valuable information, leadership opportunities, and community support on their path to pharmacy school and beyond.

What We Do

We host networking events, provide mentorship, share professional development opportunities, and build a supportive community for pre-pharmacy students at UC Merced.
